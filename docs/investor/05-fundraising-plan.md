# Cortex AI — Fundraising Plan

## Round planning

**Illustrative target:** $500,000 pre-seed  
**Target runway:** ~18 months  
**Purpose:** reach measurable product-market validation milestones.

Carta's latest U.S. pre-seed guide says most pre-seed rounds fall between $150k and $1M and recommends sizing the round around the milestones the capital buys. Carta's Q2 2026 data reports a $276k average pre-seed instrument and continued concentration of capital in AI.

Sources:
- https://carta.com/learn/startups/fundraising/pre-seed-funding/
- https://carta.com/data/state-of-pre-seed-q2-2026/

## Milestones funded by the round

### Product
- stable production release pipeline
- better onboarding completion
- stronger observability
- deeper model-cost routing
- enterprise-grade admin controls

### Traction
- first consistent paid cohort
- repeatable activation funnel
- measurable retention
- initial customer case studies

### Economics
- actual provider cost per active workspace
- gross margin by plan
- CAC by channel
- CAC payback
- contribution margin

### Distribution
- one repeatable acquisition channel
- vertical-specific landing pages
- partner/reseller experiments
- outbound sales playbook

## Investor process

### Phase A — preparation
Complete:
- production release
- traction instrumentation
- financial model
- deck
- data room
- demo script

### Phase B — evidence
Collect:
- first customer cohort
- paid conversion
- retention
- COGS
- testimonials

### Phase C — outreach
Run a structured pipeline:
**Target → Warm intro → First meeting → Demo → Diligence → Partner/IC → Term sheet**

Track dates and outcomes.

### Phase D — diligence
Answer consistently using the data room. Any uncertain number should be explicitly labeled as an assumption.

## Fundraising narrative

The narrative should not be:
> "We have an AI app and the AI market is huge."

It should be:
> "Teams need company-specific AI to operate on their own knowledge and channels. Cortex packages the operational layer required to deploy it safely and economically. The product is production-capable; this round is to prove repeatable customer demand, retention and unit economics."

## Financing instrument

In the U.S. venture ecosystem, SAFEs remain the dominant pre-seed instrument: Carta reported that 93% of pre-seed rounds in Q2 2026 used SAFEs. The exact instrument, jurisdiction and terms require qualified legal advice and should match the company's actual incorporation and investor base.

Source:
- https://carta.com/uk/en/data/safe-valuation-caps-q2-2026/

## Critical rule

Do not start broad investor outreach until the release is green and the traction sheet has at least one real cohort of users moving through activation and payment.
