# Cortex AI — One-Page Investor Summary

**What it is:** A multi-tenant AI knowledge-agent SaaS for turning company information into configurable, deployable AI agents.

**Core workflow:** Knowledge → Agent → Playground → API/Telegram → Analytics → Billing.

**Who it is for:** Initial ICP hypotheses include customer-support-heavy businesses, service companies with repeatable knowledge workflows, Telegram-centric businesses and teams with repetitive document/FAQ operations.

**Current product capabilities:** Company Brain, Agent Studio, persistent playground, tenant-scoped retrieval, managed model catalog, credit-based billing, analytics, authenticated APIs, Telegram access and Cloudflare/Neon/R2 production architecture.

**Business model:** Monthly plans + credit top-ups. Current catalog prices are Launch 3.9M Toman, Growth 12.9M Toman, Scale 24.9M Toman and Enterprise contract pricing.

**Round planning case:** $500k pre-seed / ~18 months. Illustrative use of funds: 45% product & engineering, 20% AI/model/infra, 20% GTM, 10% legal/finance/ops, 5% contingency.

**What must be proven next:** activation, retention, paid conversion, actual provider COGS, gross margin, CAC and one repeatable acquisition channel.

**Important:** Current product/catalog facts are repository-backed. Traction and financial projections in the attached model are not historical actuals unless explicitly populated from production data.
