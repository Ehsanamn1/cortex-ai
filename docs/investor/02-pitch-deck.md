# Cortex AI — Pitch Deck Copy

## Slide 1 — Cortex AI
**The operating layer for company-specific AI agents**

Turn company knowledge into deployable AI agents — configure, ground, monitor, bill and connect them to real channels.

**Stage:** Pre-seed  
**Fundraising planning case:** $500k

---

## Slide 2 — The problem
AI access is easy. Operational AI is not.

Teams still have to assemble:
- company knowledge ingestion
- retrieval and grounding
- agent behavior and memory
- permissions and tenant isolation
- model routing and cost controls
- analytics and deployment channels

The result is fragmented tooling, unpredictable costs and technical maintenance.

---

## Slide 3 — The solution
**Cortex is one control plane for production AI agents.**

**Knowledge → Agent → Playground → Channel → Analytics → Billing**

A workspace can:
- ingest company knowledge
- define agent behavior
- test the agent persistently
- expose it through API/Telegram
- measure usage and cost
- control access by plan and credit

### Business Operations

### Channels
Playground, authenticated APIs and Telegram.

---

## Slide 5 — Why Cortex is differentiated
Cortex is designed around the operational problems that appear after the first AI demo:

**Grounding** — answers can be tied to company knowledge.

**Control** — quotas, billing, access and model availability are enforced server-side.

**Deployment** — the same agent can move from testing to API/Telegram.

**Economics** — request reservations and model-aware credits make model spend measurable.

**Multi-tenancy** — data and permissions are workspace-scoped.

This is a product thesis; customer retention and usage must validate it.

## Slide 9 — Unit economics
The business should be managed around:

**ARPA – AI/model COGS – storage/infra COGS = gross contribution**

Then compare contribution against CAC.

Key equations:
- Gross Margin = 1 − COGS / Revenue
- CAC Payback (months) = CAC / monthly contribution per customer
- Net Revenue Retention = starting recurring revenue + expansion − contraction − churn / starting recurring revenue

The initial financial model uses editable assumptions rather than pretending these metrics are already validated.
