## Investor FAQ

### Why not just use ChatGPT directly?
Cortex is designed for the operational layer: company-specific knowledge, repeatable agent behavior, access controls, deployment channels, usage limits and billing.

### Is Cortex building its own foundation model?
No. Cortex is model-agnostic and manages third-party model infrastructure behind a product-level control plane. The differentiation thesis is the operational layer and customer workflow.

### What proves traction?
Production usage, activation, retention, paid workspaces, revenue and gross margin. No investor-facing traction number should be presented until verified from production data.

### How much are you raising?
The current planning case is $500k for approximately 18 months of runway. This is an internal planning target and should be finalized after real operating costs and financing structure are confirmed.

### What will the money accomplish?
Product reliability, instrumentation, customer acquisition experiments, model/infrastructure economics, enterprise readiness and measurable validation.

### What is the biggest risk?
The primary business risk is not whether the product can be built; it is whether a repeatable ICP will activate, retain and pay at attractive unit economics. The fundraising plan therefore emphasizes evidence rather than feature count.
